Understanding BMI

Useful context for interpreting your result.

📐 How BMI works

Adult BMI is calculated using weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

📊 A screening measure

BMI does not directly measure body fat, muscle, bone mass or fat distribution.

🧒 Children are different

Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age interpretation rather than adult cutoffs.

❤️ Look at the bigger picture

Health can also involve activity, nutrition, sleep, medical history and other measurements.

🏋️ Muscle matters

People with substantial muscle mass can have a higher BMI without having the same body-fat level.

🤰 Pregnancy

BMI should not be interpreted using the normal adult framework during pregnancy.

Important health information:
FitFamilyHub provides general educational information. BMI is a screening measure and is not a diagnosis. For children and teenagers, BMI should be interpreted using an appropriate age- and sex-specific growth reference. For personal medical questions or concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
